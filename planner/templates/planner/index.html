US FUEL PLANNER · API v1

Plan the drive. Price the fuel.

POST USA start and finish locations to receive a verified road route, fuel stops, purchase quantities, trip cost, and a map.

POST /api/v1/plan/
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "start": "Los Angeles, CA",
  "finish": "Dallas, TX",
  "initial_fuel_gallons": 50,
  "initial_fuel_price_usd": 3.65
}

The vehicle has a 500-mile range, a 50-gallon tank, and achieves 10 mpg. Starting fuel defaults to a full tank. When its price is omitted, the nearest station in the imported dataset supplies an explicitly labeled estimate.

Use the returned map_url to open the route. Selected stations are checked against actual road distances, within a maximum of two routing calls. Costs are optimal for the selected ordered waypoints; choosing those waypoints is a heuristic.
